Ideal Averages

Your average in one event, matched to every other event using the current world records.

Loading records…

How it works: your time is divided by the world record in your event to get a number. Every other event's world record is multiplied by that same number to give your ideal time there. It's a rough guide to where you'd be if you were equally strong everywhere, not a prediction. Records come from the official WCA results export, checked every day. This is an unofficial fan-made tool.
